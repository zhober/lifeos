# Health reference knowledge

Keep reusable external evidence with title, URL, source level, lookup date, population/applicability, and limitations. Link shared cross-domain references from experts/shared/knowledge.md. Personal observations and model hypotheses belong in sourced records/memory/cases.

## References

For each decisive claim use docs/domains/HEALTH.md and templates/health-source.json. Retain source authority, claim/outcome certainty and rating basis, applicability, exact original source, reading scope, version/correction status, limitations and conflicts, plus a reasoned use decision. Published guideline recommendation strength and evidence certainty remain separate. Optional verified journal metrics do not substitute for study appraisal. Link shared appraisals rather than creating duplicate evidence.
